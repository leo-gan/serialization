# Methodology

Compare two rows only after reading the procedure that produced them. That procedure lives on the benchmark site.

The benchmark site is the source for the rules:

- [Analysis methodology](https://leo-gan.github.io/GLD.SerializerBenchmark/analysis/ANALYSIS_METHODOLOGY/)
- [Metrics catalog](https://leo-gan.github.io/GLD.SerializerBenchmark/analysis/METRICS/)

The Mojo runner's page says those times cannot be ranked against another language. Compare a Mojo codec with other Mojo codecs.

[Results](results.md) lists the measurements this hub refers to. [Cross-format comparison](comparison.md) is about format properties, not about timings.
